Questions

Is DayFlow really free?

Yes. Everything you can do in the app today is free, with no account and no time limit. A paid "Plus" tier is planned, but it doesn't exist yet and nothing is for sale.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser, on your own device. DayFlow has no server and no accounts, so your tasks never leave your device unless you choose to export a backup. Because it's local, clearing your browser data will remove it — use the Export button in Settings to keep a safe copy.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There's no sign-up and no personal information required. Open the app and start planning.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. DayFlow is designed mobile-first, with a bottom navigation bar on phones and a sidebar on larger screens. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

Will my data sync between devices?

Not yet. Sync is a planned Plus feature and has not been built. For now you can move your data between devices manually using Export and Import in Settings.

Is this finished?

It's an honest first version built to be tested with real people. Feedback is genuinely welcome — it shapes what gets built next.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics scripts, no cookies, no tracking, no third-party services. Your data stays in your browser. Feedback is always optional and is never sent automatically. You can export or delete everything at any time from Settings.
